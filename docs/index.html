    How to read this map
    1. The ground is the Berlin biotope map: the greener, the more flowers for bees. Streets and roofs are pale.
    2. Trees are drawn one by one from the street and park tree register. They turn honey-coloured in the months they flower, if they are good for bees (lime in June–July, black locust in May, maple in April …).
    3. Zoomed out, the honeycomb lies on the ground: cells 30 m wide, bigger and darker where the model sends more bee traffic. Dark dots are wild bees people have actually recorded. Violet rings: the missing links. Zoom in and the honeycomb fades into the detailed city.
    4. Switch the bee, the month and today / + 25 bee stops to see the network change.
    Method

    Flowers and nests. Every one of the 1,675 biotope types gets a forage and a nesting score (0–1) and its flowering months, starting from a pre-classification and adding the urban types (green courtyards, allotments, railway land, streets). Street and park trees add flowers by genus and month (bee-forage lists). Sealed ground (Berlin sealing map 2021) and buildings (OSM) remove nesting ground.

    Links. The 10 m grid becomes a network of resistors: sealed ground, tall buildings and cells without flowers slow a bee down. 3,600 pairs of flower-rich cells are connected one at a time and the current through every cell is summed (circuit theory, as in Circuitscape). Each pair counts as much as both ends flower that month and as the bee's range allows (300 m for small solitary bees, 1 km for bumblebees).

    Bee stops. For small bees, flower patches whose gap is 100 m or less form one network. Each candidate bus or tram stop gets a 30 m × 30 m flower bed (April–September); the 25 that join the most network area are chosen one by one (equivalent connected area). The circuit model is then run again with them.

    Limits

    This is a model of potential, not measured nectar or verified flight routes. Scores are expert-style rules and need checking on the ground. Wild bees differ by species: some need one plant, some sand, some dead wood.

    No record does not mean no bees: records cluster where people walk (parks, Tempelhofer Feld). Locations accurate to 100 m or better only; obscured records and the honey bee are left out. Records without an open licence are counted but not shown as points.
